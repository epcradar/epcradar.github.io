How we verify

Every post carries its confidence level

We publish only what is already public, and we say how sure we are. No rumours dressed up as news, no letters of intent passed off as contracts.

CONFIRMED

Official source: a company statement or a stock exchange filing.

REPORTED

Credible trade press, named in the post. Not yet confirmed by the parties.

ON THE RADAR

An award we expect, usually after a final investment decision. A forecast, not a fact.

We never ask for money for jobs

Not for a listing, not for an introduction, not for a "processing fee". Every vacancy we link points to the employer's own careers page or to a licensed agency we have checked.

If anyone contacts you using our name and asks for payment, it is not us. Tell us.

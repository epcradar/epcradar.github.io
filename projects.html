PROJECT RADAR

The five projects hiring hardest right now

These five are past the drawings and into construction. Between them they are the biggest concentration of oil and gas hiring on the planet this year.

You apply to the contractor, not to the client. Nobody is hired by ADNOC to build Hail & Ghasha — they are hired by Tecnimont, or by NMDC Energy. So for each project below we name the EPC contractor first, every member of the joint venture, and each partner's share where the company itself published it. Percentages inside a JV are almost never disclosed; a partner's own slice in dollars usually is, and we say whose number it is rather than dividing one figure by another and calling the result a stake.

Every contractor below is named in a company or joint-venture statement we link to, alongside the employer's own careers page.

Updated 24 October 2026

Before you apply — read this

Every project on this page is real, and that is exactly what makes it useful to someone trying to cheat you. Fake recruiters read the same news we do and use these names as bait. The project is real. The award is real. The recruiter is not.

  • No legitimate employer charges you for a visa, a medical, a ticket, training, or a "processing fee". Not before, not after, not ever.
  • An offer letter that arrives before any interview is not an offer letter.
  • A recruiter writing from Gmail, Outlook or WhatsApp instead of a company domain is not that company.
  • "Pay today or you lose the slot" is the whole scam in one sentence.

Apply on the pages linked below and nowhere else. Several of these employers publish their own recruitment-fraud warnings on those same pages — McDermott states plainly that it never uses agencies that charge candidates fees, and NMDC Energy that it never asks for payment or bank details at any stage. Read them.

01 · Mozambique

Mozambique LNG

CONFIRMED
TotalEnergies · Afungi, Cabo Delgado

The project that hires the most people, and the one that says so out loud. TotalEnergies expects the construction phase to create up to 7,000 direct jobs for Mozambicans. First LNG is expected in 2029.

Where it stands · October 2026

In July TotalEnergies' chief executive put the project at almost 45% complete, with 7,000 to 8,000 people working on it, up from about 4,000 at the January restart. First LNG is still targeted for 2029. REPORTED: Club of Mozambique, Q2 results call.

02 · Qatar

North Field East & South

CONFIRMED
QatarEnergy · Ras Laffan

Two separate expansions, two separate joint ventures — a distinction worth getting right, because they hire separately. North Field East is four mega trains of 8 mtpa each plus carbon capture, built by the Chiyoda–Technip Energies joint venture. North Field South is two more trains, 16 mtpa, built by Technip Energies with Consolidated Contractors Company.

Where it stands · October 2026

North Field East: Reuters reports the first train will be ready to start in November, with production in the first quarter of 2027; QatarEnergy's chief executive says the first half of 2027. For workers this means commissioning and operations roles, while construction crews start to demobilise. New: North Field West, two more 8 MTPA trains, was awarded on 25 February 2026 to a joint venture of Technip Energies (leader), Consolidated Contractors Company and Gulf Asia Contracting. Sources: Reuters via EnergyNow, 7 Oct 2026 · Technip Energies, 25 Feb 2026.

03 · Saudi Arabia

Jafurah

REPORTED
Saudi Aramco · Eastern Province

The kingdom's largest unconventional gas development, and the one where you have to do the most work yourself. Aramco does not name its EPC contractors — it announces awards in aggregate, which is why this project is marked REPORTED and the others are not. Every firm below named itself, in its own statement.

Where it stands · October 2026

Gas production at Jafurah started in December 2025, Aramco announced on 26 February 2026. Phase two is under construction: 16 contracts worth about USD 12.4bn for compression, new gas processing trains, utilities, sulphur and export facilities. Source: Aramco, 26 Feb 2026.

04 · United Arab Emirates

Hail & Ghasha

CONFIRMED
ADNOC · offshore and onshore Abu Dhabi

A USD 17 billion EPC programme split cleanly in two, which tells you exactly where to apply. Offshore — drilling centres, a processing plant on artificial islands and hundreds of kilometres of subsea pipeline — went to Saipem and NMDC Energy. Onshore — carbon dioxide and sulphur recovery and handling — went to Tecnimont alone, the largest award in its parent group's history.

Where it stands · October 2026

Offshore, the first two steel jackets were installed in July 2025 by NMDC Energy's vessels. ADNOC raised about USD 11bn of financing in December 2025. Onshore at Ruwais, Tecnimont is now hiring commissioning staff for November 2026 starts: our read is that the onshore plant is moving from construction towards commissioning. Sources: Offshore Energy, July 2025 · The National, Dec 2025.

05 · United Arab Emirates

Ruwais LNG

CONFIRMED
ADNOC · Al Ruwais Industrial City

Two trains, 9.6 mtpa, designed to run on electric drives rather than gas turbines. The EPC went to the TJN Ruwais joint venture — Technip Energies as leader, with JGC and NMDC Energy. NMDC puts the contract at USD 5.5 billion; Technip Energies discloses only its own share, above one billion euros. That gap is normal, and worth knowing when you read contract values anywhere.

Under construction. The first train is scheduled for the second half of 2028 and the second for early 2029, according to ADNOC Gas' plan to take a 60% stake in the plant. Source: World Oil, Nov 2024.

How to check a recruiter in four minutes

  1. Start from the company, not the message. Open the careers page above yourself and look for the role there. If it does not exist on the employer's own site, it does not exist.
  2. Check the email domain, character by character. A single swapped letter is the oldest trick in this business.
  3. Ask any agency for its licence number and check it with your country's labour authority. A legitimate agency gives it to you without hesitating. That reaction is the test.
  4. Never pay. For anything. Once money has moved, there is usually no getting it back and no one to complain to.
